## 2. Proposed Architecture & Scope

We propose a phased implementation built specifically to resolve the operational bottlenecks identified in our audit.

```
Phase 1: Organize (Systems) --> Unify the internal database, CRM, and SOPs.
Phase 2: Articulate (Signal)  --> Refine messaging, keyword maps, and marketing.
Phase 3: Project (Front)     --> Design a premium, high-conversion web interface.
```

### 2.1 Practice I: Bureau Systems (The Operational Machine)
We will build a custom, integrated operational hub on Notion tailored to your team's workflow.
*   **Flagship CRM**: A single repository for lead tracking, customer history, proposal status, and contact detail organization.
*   **Project & Task Command**: A unified team board to assign tasks, set deadlines, and track active client delivery status in real time.
*   **SOP Wiki & Resource Library**: A central directory hosting standard operating procedures, brand guidelines, and contract templates to ensure consistency across the team.

### 2.2 Practice II: Bureau Signal (The Brand Voice)
We will craft the strategic framework to establish your authority in the market.
*   **Topical Authority Audit**: Researching search intent and compiling a targeted keyword map for your niche.
*   **Content Blueprint**: A 3-month strategy and publication calendar containing specific positioning briefs.

### 2.3 Practice III: Bureau Front (The Digital Face)
We will create a premium web presence that represents the actual quality of your business.
*   **Interface Build**: A responsive website featuring clean layout hierarchies, custom typography, and optimized conversion elements. Built on Wix (for client-managed ease), Framer (for visual aesthetics & micro-animations), or custom static HTML/CSS/JS (for raw speed).
*   **Technical Deployment**: Domain mapping, SSL activation, Core Web Vitals optimization, and server configurations (Vercel, GoDaddy, or native hosts).
