# Bureau — Pitch Deck Concepts & Copy
*Confidential strategic asset of Bureau. Outlining the slide-by-slide structure, copywriting, and visual guidelines for pitching premium clients.*

---

## 1. Visual & Aesthetic Guidelines
To match the quiet luxury of the Bureau brand, any presentation slides created from these concepts must follow these rules:
*   **Background**: Strict dark mode. Pure black (#000000) or deep obsidian (#050505).
*   **Typography**: Serif titles (e.g., Fondamento or a premium editorial serif) with clean, light sans-serif body text (e.g., Inter or Outfit, 300/400 weight).
*   **Colors**: Crimson Red (#FF2D2D) used sparingly as an accent (e.g., small lines, numbers, or key metrics). Bone White (#F5F5F0) for primary text. Muted grey for supporting text.
*   **Layout**: Maximum whitespace. Avoid stock illustrations, complex icons, or clip art. Use clean typographic layouts, large high-contrast quotes, and screenshot mockups of actual custom workspaces.
*   **Length**: Maximum 8–9 slides. Focus on clarity over volume.
