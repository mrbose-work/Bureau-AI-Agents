# Bureau: The Brand Bible

## Philosophy
Bureau is not just an agency; it's a modular ecosystem for the future of business. We prioritize zero-cost foundations with premium-tier results.

## Visual Language
- **Theme**: Obsidian & Neon.
- **Colors**: 
  - Primary: #050505 (Obsidian)
  - Accent A: #00e5ff (Flow Blue)
  - Accent B: #ffd700 (Stress Gold)
- **Typography**: Inter (Body), Outfit (Headings).

## Core Values
1. **Agentic Autonomy**: Empowering businesses with AI that thinks and acts.
2. **Technical Superiority**: Leading with SEO and modern web stacks.
3. **Sleek Simplicity**: High complexity under the hood, clean beauty on the surface.
